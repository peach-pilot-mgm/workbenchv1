The brief

Three nights. Three cities. One purpose.

Get the group out of Atlanta for a proper guys’ weekend: competitive padel during the day, strong dinners at night and enough hotel quality to make the short trip feel like a real escape.

Editor’s choice

Costa Rica

Panama is cheaper, but Costa Rica offers the strongest overall weekend: nonstop Delta service, a true Marriott resort, Alvaro’s local connections and the most compelling padel setup.
