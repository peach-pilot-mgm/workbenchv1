The Brief

Three nights, eight friends, and three cities competing to host the best padel weekend of the year. The goal is simple: strong courts during the day, memorable restaurants at night, and a hotel worth returning to between both.

Nº 1

Editor’s Choice

Costa Rica

Panama is the least expensive option, but Costa Rica offers the strongest overall weekend: nonstop Delta service, a true Marriott resort, Alvaro’s local connections, and the best expected padel experience.

Costa Rica is approximately $164 more per person than Panama when sharing a room.

Top Recommendation

The Hotel

Costa Rica Marriott Hotel Hacienda Belén

A resort-style Marriott set on a historic hacienda estate — coffee-plantation grounds, arcaded courtyards, pool, spa, and several restaurants — minutes from SJO airport. Alvaro lives locally and can open doors to clubs, restaurants, transportation, and local access all weekend.

  • Resort-style Marriott property with historic hacienda atmosphere
  • Pool, spa, restaurants, and gardens on the estate
  • Close to SJO airport — short transfer on arrival and departure

The Recommendation

The Winner: Costa Rica

Panama is the value play, and Mexico City remains the strongest food-and-nightlife destination. But Costa Rica offers the best total weekend: nonstop travel, the Marriott resort setting, Alvaro’s local access, and the strongest expected padel experience.

At approximately $164 more per person than Panama when sharing a room, Costa Rica earns the premium.
